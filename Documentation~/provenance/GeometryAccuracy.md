# Android frame and safe-area accuracy

Audit date: 2026-10-25. This audit covers the 59 profiles added in the catalog expansion.

30 profiles now use official native artwork (28 Samsung through a pinned public mirror, 2 directly from Google). Exterior Samsung poster cropping remains fitted; separate new side-button sprites are not included. 24 profiles use matched physical/RTL cutout measurements, 2 use emulator captures, 8 use native-mask exclusions, 3 use community ROM configuration, and 22 remain unverified. Frame fidelity and safe-area evidence are independent.

Safe rectangles below are immersive projections with system bars hidden; they are not the source site's system-bars-visible rectangles. Pixel captures are emulator evidence. Native scaling is explicit. All absent capture orientations are derived test cases, not measured rotations. Existing 33 profiles are outside this geometry upgrade's scope.

## Rejected or limited sources

- Galaxy A13 5G's archived official skin layout is 1080 × 2408, whereas the target is 720 × 1600. It was rejected; the frame stays approximate. SM-A136B inset captures were accepted.
- Source directories labelled A14/A15/A16 5G contain SM-A145F/SM-A155F/SM-A165N captures: those are applied only to the LTE profiles. They are not reused as 5G measurements.
- A17's source capture is SM-A175N (LTE); A07's is SM-A076M (5G). Neither matches the corresponding catalog target, so neither was applied.
- A25 5G has an official Samsung download listing, but its ZIP requires sign-in and the reviewed public mirror lacks that model. Its official frame remains pending; the safe area now uses an explicitly labelled community ROM configuration.
- Galaxy S23 Ultra, S24+, S24 Ultra and S25 Edge captures use lower rendering resolutions. Native-panel profiles retain their resolution and explicitly scale the measured coordinates; this needs on-device confirmation at native resolution.
- No additional exact-model official skins or physical inset captures were found in the follow-up search. POCO F7 and Redmi 13C now use community ROM cutout configuration. See [the follow-up research](GeometryResearch.md) for all remaining profiles and rejected candidates.

Raw measurements and source hashes are preserved. `ValidateGeometrySources.py` verifies 72 orientations against those captures, including LTE/5G identity and Android-to-Unity rotation/coordinate conversion. Unity validation checks all 92 Android profiles in all four orientations (368 checks); this validates simulation consistency, not universal physical-device behavior.
